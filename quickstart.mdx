Use this path when you want to create a festival and begin setup.

## Prerequisites

- You can sign in with Google or create a Festie account.
- You know the festival name, motto, and preferred public link.
- You are the owner or an admin for the festival.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open Festie and sign in with Google, or create an account with email and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the festival">
    Choose the festival creation option. Select the standard festival setup shown in the product flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add festival details">
    Enter the festival name, motto, preferred link, and the required setup fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the trial">
    Start the free trial to use the main features before choosing a paid plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Go to `Festival > Home`, then open the created subfest dashboard from the festival area.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  After the dashboard opens, follow the admin setup pages in order. Festie shows pending tasks on the dashboard so you can see what remains.
</Tip>
