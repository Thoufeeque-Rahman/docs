---
title: "Create your first festival"
description: "Create a festival, select the setup type, choose a link, and start managing your event."
---

Once you log in, you will be directed to the dashboard where you can create your very first festival. This must be done before you can add categories, teams, candidates, or programmes.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in to your Festie account.
- You know the festival's name, motto, and preferred subdomain (custom link).

## Festival Setup Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start festival creation">
    From your dashboard, click on the **New Festival** button. If you have already created festivals, they will be listed here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the festival type">
    Festie asks for the type of festival you are hosting. You have three options:
    
    1. **Single Standalone Fest:** Select this if you are running a single, unified festival (e.g., an Arts Fest only) at a school, madrasa, or college.
    2. **One Main Festival with Multiple Sub Fests:** Select this if your event includes various distinct festivals (e.g., an Arts Fest, a Sports Fest, and other cultural events) running under one main umbrella festival.
    3. **Multi Level Festival:** Select this if your festival spans across multiple geographical or organizational levels (e.g., Zone, District, State levels).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter identity details">
    Add the fundamental details for your festival:
    - **Name:** The official name of your festival.
    - **Motto:** The festival's motto or slogan (you can leave this blank if you don't have one).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your Subdomain">
    Choose the link format for your public website. 
    
    By default, you can define how the public accesses your site. For example, if you enter `26`, your public festival link will be `26.festie.app`. Modify this to suit your institution's name or event abbreviation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Contact Details">
    Provide your contact information. You can use the search option to quickly find and select your address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish Creation">
    Once you submit the form, your festival will be created and you will be taken to the festival's management interface. You will start on a Free Trial plan which you can upgrade later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a short, readable subdomain. This link becomes part of the route participants and the public use to reach your festival's website.
</Tip>
