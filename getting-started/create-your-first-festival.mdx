---
title: "Create your first festival"
description: "Create a madrasah festival, choose a link, and start the trial."
---

Create the festival before you add categories, teams, candidates, programmes, and results.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in to Festie.
- You know the festival name, motto, and preferred subdomain or link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start festival creation">
    From the authenticated area, choose the option to create a festival.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the setup type">
    For a madrasah festival, select the single standard festival setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter identity details">
    Add the festival or madrasah name, motto, and link exactly as you want them to appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete required fields">
    Fill the remaining setup fields shown in the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the trial">
    Select the free trial to continue into the dashboard. You can upgrade later from `Festival > Billing` or `Subfest > Billing`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a short, readable link. This link becomes part of the route people use to reach the festival and public website.
</Tip>
