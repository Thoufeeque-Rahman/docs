title: "Navigation map"
description: "Understand Festival and Subfest navigation in Festie."
---

Festie has two main navigation levels.

## Festival level

Use Festival pages for owner-level setup and account-level actions.

- `Festival > Home` shows the owner dashboard.
- `Festival > Fests` lists and creates subfests.
- `Festival > Users` manages festival-level sharing and roles.
- `Festival > Resource` manages shared candidates, programmes, and files.
- `Festival > Website` manages the festival website area.
- `Festival > Billing` manages plans and payment.
- `Festival > Settings` manages festival settings.

## Subfest level

Use Subfest pages for the actual event workflow.

- `Subfest > Dashboard` shows setup status and analytics.
- `Subfest > Candidate` manages candidates, teams, sections, and candidate analytics.
- `Subfest > Programme` manages programmes, collections, skills, curbs, and programme analytics.
- `Subfest > Registrations` manages candidate, topic, shared candidate, and substitution registrations.
- `Subfest > Schedule` manages the calendar, venues, judges, and conflicts.
- `Subfest > Results` manages result entry, releases, point adjustments, toppers, and analytics.
- `Subfest > Content`, `Subfest > Templates`, and `Subfest > Website` manage public-facing material.
- `Subfest > Reports` provides report and export pages.

<Note>
  Route examples use `/:festivalSubdomain/:subFestSlug/...`. Replace those placeholders with your festival link and subfest slug.
</Note>
