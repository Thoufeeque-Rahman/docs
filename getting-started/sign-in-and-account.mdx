---
title: "Sign in and account"
description: "Sign in to Festie with Google or create an account before joining a festival."
---

You need a Festie account before you can create a festival or receive access as a team manager, judge, or media user.

## Prerequisites

- Use a browser where you can access your email or Google account.
- If an admin is adding you to a festival, use the same email address they will invite.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the login page">
    Go to the Festie login page from the dashboard link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a sign-in method">
    Select Google login, or create an account with email and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete authentication">
    Follow the prompts until Festie opens your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share your email if needed">
    If you are joining as a team manager, judge, or media user, share the signed-in email address with the festival admin.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Admins can add a user only after that person has an account in Festie.
</Note>
