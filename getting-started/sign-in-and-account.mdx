---
title: "Sign in and account"
description: "Sign in to Festie with Google or create an account before joining a festival."
---

You need a Festie account before you can create a new festival or receive access as a team manager, judge, or media user.

## Prerequisites

- Use a browser where you can access your email or Google account.
- If an admin is adding you to a festival, use the exact same email address they will invite.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the login page">
    Go to the Festie login page to start the authentication process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a sign-in method">
    Festie provides two easy ways to log in:
    - **Google Login:** Quickly log in using your Google account.
    - **Create Account:** If you prefer to use an email and password, click on the **Create Account** option, provide your name and details, and register.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete authentication">
    Follow the prompts until Festie opens your account dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share your email if needed">
    If you are joining as a team manager, judge, or media user, share your registered email address with the festival admin so they can grant you access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Admins can add a user only after that person has successfully created an account in Festie.
</Note>
