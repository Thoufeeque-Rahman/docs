---
title: "Roles and access"
description: "Review what each role can access in Festie."
---

Festie access depends on the user's assigned role and the current festival or subfest.

## Owner and primary user

Owners and primary users manage festival-level pages such as:

- `Festival > Home`
- `Festival > Fests`
- `Festival > Users`
- `Festival > Resource`
- `Festival > Website`
- `Festival > Billing`
- `Festival > Settings`

## Admin

Admins manage the full subfest workflow:

- Dashboard, candidates, teams, sections, programmes, curbs, registrations, schedules, results, reports, templates, website, notifications, users, and billing where available.

## Team manager

Team managers work with their assigned team:

- Candidate and registration areas available to the team role.
- Topic registration, substitutions, notifications, and appeals where enabled.
- Team-related results and information.

## Media

Media users can access content and template areas such as:

- `Subfest > Content > Files`
- `Subfest > Content > Gallery`
- `Subfest > Content > Downloads`
- `Subfest > Content > News`
- `Subfest > Templates`

## Judge

Judges access assigned online judgement routes:

- Programme list.
- Programme instructions.
- Programme candidates.
- Score columns.
- Candidate score entry.

<Note>
  Exact visible pages can also depend on subscription features and festival configuration.
</Note>
