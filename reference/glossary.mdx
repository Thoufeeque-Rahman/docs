---
title: "Glossary"
description: "Definitions for common Festie terms."
---

## Festival

The top-level event workspace. It contains owner-level pages, billing, users, website settings, and one or more subfests.

## Subfest

The event unit where candidates, programmes, registrations, schedules, results, and reports are managed.

## Candidate

A participant or student who competes in programmes.

## Team

A group candidates belong to for registration and scoring.

## Category

A level or age group used to organize candidates and programmes.

## Section

An extra classification such as class, stream, or department.

## Programme

A competition inside a subfest. A programme can be individual or group.

## Collection

A grouping of programmes, such as Stage or Non-stage.

## Curb

A participation limit that controls how many programmes a candidate can join by category, programme category, or collection.

## Topic registration

The process of submitting a topic, title, or description for a registered candidate or group.

## Release

A published or unpublished batch of result changes.

## Topper

A leading team or candidate based on points.
