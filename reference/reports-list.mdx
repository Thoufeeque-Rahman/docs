---

Open `Subfest > Reports`.

## Available reports

- Programme curb report: review programme-level curb compliance.
- Team curb report: review team-level limit compliance.
- Programme result report: review programme results, published or unpublished.
- Candidate points report: review each candidate's points.
- Toppers report: review top teams or candidates.
- Programme registrations report: review registrations by programme.
- Programme participation report: review participation counts and coverage.
- Topic registrations report: review submitted topics.
- Substitutions report: review participant substitutions.
- Programme report: review full programme details.
- Candidate report: review full candidate details.
- Exports: download generated export files.

<Tip>
  Add templates before downloading formatted reports when you need a specific certificate, poster, or sheet design.
</Tip>
