---
title: "Gallery and downloads"
description: "Publish gallery images and downloadable files on the public website."
---

Gallery and downloads help you share event material with participants and visitors.

## Prerequisites

- The website is enabled.
- You have admin or media access.
- Files or images are ready to upload.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open content">
    Go to `Subfest > Content > Gallery` or `Subfest > Content > Downloads`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add items">
    Upload images to gallery albums or add downloadable files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check website settings">
    Go to `Subfest > Website` and confirm gallery or downloads are enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the website">
    Open the public website preview and confirm the content appears correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>
