---
title: "Content files"
description: "Manage files, gallery, downloads, and news for a subfest."
---

The content area stores public and internal material for the subfest.

## Prerequisites

- You have admin or media access.
- You know which files should be published or shared.

Open `Subfest > Content`.

Use the content pages for:

- `Subfest > Content > Files`
- `Subfest > Content > Gallery`
- `Subfest > Content > Downloads`
- `Subfest > Content > News`

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload files">
    Add files under the files area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize gallery items">
    Add albums or gallery images when needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare downloads">
    Add downloadable files for users or website visitors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish news">
    Create news items when your website uses news updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>
