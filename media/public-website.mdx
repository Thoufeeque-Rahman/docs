---
title: "Public Website & Wall"
description: "Launch, customize, and manage your festival's public-facing website."
---

Every festival in Festie gets its own dedicated public website where participants and audiences can view schedules, results, and live updates. 

Navigate to `Festival > Website` to manage your online presence.

## 1. Overview & Launching
- **Analytics:** View how many people are visiting your site, which devices they are using, and from which countries.
- **Domain:** Manage your festival's custom subdomain (e.g., `yourfest.festie.com`).
- **Launch:** Click **Launch** to make your website live to the public.

---

## 2. Customizing Pages (Home, About, Gallery)

Go to the **Pages** section to edit the content of your website.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Home Page Text">
    Update the main headers, descriptions, and statistics (e.g., change "250+ Programmes" to match your actual numbers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hero Images">
    Upload a **Desktop Hero Image** (a wide banner) and a **Mobile Hero Image** (a taller, narrower version) for the top of your homepage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="About & Contact Details">
    Add an 'About Us' description and image, and update your public contact information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gallery">
    Upload photos to the homepage gallery to showcase previous events or current highlights.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes** and refresh your public website to see the updates instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## 3. Theme Customization (Colors & Logos)

To change the overall look and feel of the website:
1. Click on **Theme Customizer**.
2. **Logos:** Upload your festival's Header Logo and Footer Logo.
3. **Colors:** Pick your primary colors (e.g., changing the default red theme to blue or green).
4. Save the changes to instantly re-theme the entire site.

---

## 4. The Live "Wall"

The **Wall** is a special page designed to be cast to a large TV or projector at your venue. It displays live, rolling updates of results and top candidates.

- You can customize what appears on the wall (e.g., toggle Candidate Toppers, Team Toppers, or Avatars on/off).
- Choose between different Wall designs.

---

## 5. Page Visibility (Hiding Pages)

If you don't want the public to see certain pages (for example, you aren't using the "News" feature or want to hide "My Result"):
1. Go to **Page Visibility**.
2. Uncheck the pages you want to hide from the Navigation Bar (Navbar) or the Footer.
3. Once unchecked, those links will disappear from your public website.
