---
title: "Public website"
description: "Enable and configure the public website for a festival or subfest."
---

The public website lets participants and visitors see festival information, schedules, results, galleries, downloads, and other enabled sections.

## Prerequisites

- You have admin access.
- Basic festival and subfest details are ready.

Open `Subfest > Website` or `Festival > Website`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the website">
    Turn on the website feature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the preview">
    Check the generated website preview and analytics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the domain or link">
    Review or update the public link created during festival setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure sections">
    Choose whether to show schedules, results, gallery, downloads, news, and other website features.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update page content">
    Edit home page title, content, contact page details, header, footer, and menu settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload images and logo">
    Add the hero image, website logo, and related images.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  After changing images or logo, return to the overview preview to confirm the website reflects the update.
</Tip>
