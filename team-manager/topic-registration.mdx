---
title: "Topic Registration"
description: "Submit and approve specific topics for programmes (e.g., Speech or Essay topics)."
---

Some programmes require candidates to register a specific topic, title, or concept note before the competition (for example, the subject of a speech). 

## Prerequisites

- Candidates must already be registered for the programme.
- The Admin must have enabled **Require Topic Registration** and **Topic Registration Open** in the Programme Settings.

Navigate to `Subfest > Registrations > Topic Registration`.

---

## Submitting a Topic

Team Managers (or Admins) can submit topics for their registered candidates:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the Programme">
    Team Managers will only see programmes where Topic Registration is currently open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Topic">
    Click on the candidate and enter the topic name or title. Add a description or supporting details if required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Save the topic. It will now be marked as "Pending Review" by the Admin.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Admin Approval Process

Admins have the authority to manage all submitted topics:
1. Admins can click **Review** on any submitted topic.
2. They can choose to **Approve** or **Reject** the topic. (If rejected, the Team Manager can submit a new one).
3. Admins can also bypass the Team Manager and directly add and instantly approve a topic themselves.

---

## Cross-Team Topic Privacy

By default, Festie allows Team Managers to see which topics other teams have chosen for a programme, to avoid duplicates.

If you want to keep topics secret:
1. Go to `Settings > Configure > General Settings`.
2. Scroll to the **Substitution and Topics** section.
3. Turn off **Allow Cross Team Topic View**.
4. Now, Team Managers will only be able to see the topics submitted by their own team.
