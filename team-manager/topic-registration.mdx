---
title: "Topic registration"
description: "Submit topics for programmes that require them."
---

Some programmes require a topic, title, or description after candidate registration.

## Prerequisites

- You have registered a candidate or group for the programme.
- The admin enabled topic registration for the programme.
- Topic registration is open for team managers.

Open `Subfest > Registrations > Topic Registration`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the programme">
    Open the topic registration row for the relevant programme and candidate or group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the topic">
    Enter the topic or title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add description if needed">
    Add supporting details when the programme requires them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Save the topic for admin review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Admins may approve topics after submission.
</Note>
