---
title: "Candidate Registration"
description: "Register candidates for programmes and generate team sheets."
---

Registration is the process of assigning candidates to specific programmes. Both Admins and Team Managers can register candidates, but they have different levels of access.

## Prerequisites

- Candidates and Programmes must be set up.
- **For Team Managers:** The Admin must have opened registration for the specific programme in the settings.

Navigate to `Subfest > Registrations` (or the specific programme's page).

---

## How to Register Candidates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a Programme">
    Choose the programme you want to register candidates for. If you are an Admin, you can select multiple programmes at once to bulk-open registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search and Select Candidates">
    Click to register, and search for the candidate's name or chest number. 
    - *Team Managers* will only be able to search and see candidates from their own team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Group Registration Rules">
    If it is a group programme, pay attention to the limits. If the programme requires a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 3 candidates, you **must** select exactly 3 candidates. If you select 2 or 4, the **Save** button will be disabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The candidates are now officially registered for that programme.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Exporting Team Sheets (Registration Lists)

Admins can download the full list of registered candidates as a PDF or Excel file. This is very useful for printing schedules or judges' sheets.

1. Click on **Team Sheets**.
2. **Filter your list:** You can filter the export by Category (e.g., Junior), Class/Section, Programme Collection (e.g., Stage events only), or by a specific Team.
3. **Include Extra Details:** Check the boxes to include the Programme Code or the Candidate's Class/Section in the final export.
4. **Export:** Click **Export PDF** or **Export Excel**.
