---
title: "Register candidates"
description: "Register team candidates for open programmes."
---

Team managers can register candidates only when the admin opens registration for a programme.

## Prerequisites

- You have team manager access.
- Candidates exist for your team.
- Registration is open for the programme.

Open `Subfest > Registrations`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a programme">
    Open the programme you want to register for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click register">
    Use the registration action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search and select candidates">
    Search for the candidate and select the correct record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the registration and check that it appears under the programme.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Admins can register at any time. Team managers need registration to be open.
</Note>
