title: "Appeals and notifications"
description: "Receive admin notifications and send appeals from the team panel."
---

Team managers can receive notifications from admins and send appeals when the feature is enabled.

## Prerequisites

- You have team manager access.
- The admin has enabled or provided the relevant notification and appeal flow.

## Notifications

Open `Subfest > Notifications`.

Use this page to read messages sent by admins to team leaders.

## Appeals

Open `Subfest > Notifications > Appeals` or the appeal request page available to your role.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start an appeal">
    Choose the request appeal action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Explain the issue clearly and include the programme or candidate details where relevant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Submit the appeal for admin review.
  </Step>
</Steps>
