---
title: "Team manager overview"
description: "Understand what team managers can do after admin access is granted."
---

Team managers handle work for one assigned team. They can register participants and review team-related information after an admin adds their account.

## Prerequisites

- You have created a Festie account.
- The admin has added your email under `Subfest > Users`.
- The admin assigned you to a team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in with the same email the admin added.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the subfest">
    Open the festival link shared by the admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use team pages">
    Use `Subfest > Registrations`, `Subfest > Notifications`, and result-related pages available to your role.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Team manager access is scoped to the assigned team.
</Note>
