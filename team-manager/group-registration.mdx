---
title: "Group registration"
description: "Register members for group programmes."
---

Group programmes require multiple candidates from the same team.

## Prerequisites

- You have team manager access.
- The group programme is open for registration.
- Your team has enough candidates for the programme's minimum member count.

Open `Subfest > Registrations`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the group programme">
    Select the group programme for your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select members">
    Choose candidates until the programme's required member count is met.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use search">
    Search when the candidate is not visible in the first list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review current members">
    Confirm who is currently in the group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or update">
    Save the group, or use add and remove actions to update members.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  You cannot save a group that does not satisfy the programme's minimum and maximum member settings.
</Warning>
