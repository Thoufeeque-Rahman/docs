---
title: "Judge programmes"
description: "View programmes assigned to a judge."
---

Judges start from the programmes list.

## Prerequisites

- You have judge access.
- An admin assigned programmes or judging work to you.

Open `Subfest > Programmes` in the judge panel.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review assigned programmes">
    Check the list of programmes available to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a programme">
    Select the programme you need to judge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read instructions">
    Open the programme instructions before entering scores.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Judge routes include programme instructions, candidates, score columns, and candidate score pages.
</Note>
