---
title: "Score entry"
description: "Enter scores from the judge panel."
---

Use score entry when a programme is configured for online judgement.

## Prerequisites

- You have judge access.
- The programme is assigned to you.
- The programme's score columns are ready.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assigned programme">
    Go to the programme from the judge programmes list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read instructions">
    Review programme instructions before scoring.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open candidates">
    Open the candidate list for the programme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter scores">
    Open a candidate and enter scores in the available columns.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Confirm score values before submitting. Score data affects result calculations.
</Warning>
