---
title: "Open registration"
description: "Open programme registration for team managers."
---

Admins can register participants at any time. Team managers can register only when registration is open for the programme.

## Prerequisites

- Programmes are created.
- Team managers have access if they will register participants.

Open `Subfest > Programme`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select programmes">
    Select one programme or multiple programmes from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open settings">
    Click the settings action for the selected programme or selected group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to registration settings">
    Open the registration section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn registration on">
    Enable registration and choose **Open now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and apply">
    Preview the changes, then apply settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Opening registration lets team managers submit entries. Confirm participation limits and curbs before opening.
</Warning>
