---
title: "Schedules"
description: "Schedule programmes with calendar or table view."
---

Use scheduling to assign programmes to times, venues, and judges.

## Prerequisites

- Programmes are created.
- Venues and judges are available.

Open `Subfest > Schedule`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add judges and venues">
    Use `Subfest > Schedule > Judges` and `Subfest > Schedule > Venues` to add or review resources.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use calendar view">
    Drag a programme onto the calendar at the correct date and time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust duration">
    Drag the programme block to change its time range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit details directly">
    Change start time, end time, venue, or judge from the schedule item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use table view when faster">
    Switch to table view to enter time, venue, and judge values in rows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The schedule route is `/:festivalSubdomain/:subFestSlug/schedule`.
</Note>
