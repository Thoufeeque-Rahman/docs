---
title: "Scheduling & Auto-Scheduling"
description: "Assign times, venues, and judges manually or use AI Auto-Scheduling."
---

Festie provides a powerful scheduling interface to organize when and where your programmes happen, and who will judge them. You can do this manually or let our AI handle it.

## Prerequisites

- Programmes are created.
- You have added your Venues and Judges in the Schedule settings.

Navigate to `Subfest > Schedule`.

---

## 1. Manual Scheduling Views

You have three main views to manually build your schedule:

### Calendar View
This is a standard calendar interface.
- **Drag and Drop:** Grab an unscheduled programme and drop it onto a time slot.
- **Adjust Duration:** Click and drag the bottom of the programme block to extend or reduce its duration.
- **Edit Details:** Click a scheduled programme to assign the Venue and Judges.

### Column View
This view shows each day as a separate column.
- **Insert Breaks:** You can click the `+` button to quickly insert a Lunch Break or Tea Break between programmes.
- **Adjust Times:** You can tweak times and see how it pushes the rest of the day's schedule down.

### Table View
If you prefer a spreadsheet-like experience, the Table view lets you rapidly type in the Start Time, Duration, Venue, and Judges row by row.

---

## 2. Assigning Judges (Lead & Substitute)

When you assign judges to a programme, you can give them specific roles:
- **Normal Judge:** A standard evaluator.
- **Lead Judge:** If there is a tie in scores, the system will prioritize the marks given by the Lead Judge to break the tie.
- **Substitute Judge:** A backup judge assigned just in case the primary judge cannot make it. If the primary judge is absent, the Admin can quickly promote the Substitute to a Normal Judge.

---

## 3. Conflict Checker

When scheduling manually, it's easy to accidentally schedule the same student in two different venues at the exact same time. 
To prevent this, go to `Registrations > Conflict Checker`. Select any two programmes, and the system will warn you if the exact same candidate or judge is booked for both simultaneously.

---

## 4. Auto-Scheduling (AI Beta)

If you don't want to schedule manually, you can use Festie's AI Auto-Scheduler.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start Auto-Scheduler">
    Click the **Auto Schedule** button. Select whether you want to schedule all unprogrammed events or just specific selected ones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Global Times">
    - Define the default duration for a programme (e.g., 30 mins).
    - Define the gap between programmes (e.g., 10 mins).
    - Define your global breaks (e.g., Lunch from 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Daily Working Hours">
    Tell the AI when the festival starts and ends each day (e.g., 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM) and select which Venues are available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Custom Rules">
    You can tell the AI specific rules, for example:
    - *Duration Rule:* "All Group programmes must be 45 minutes long."
    - *Venue Rule:* "All Senior Category programmes MUST happen at Stage A."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Provide any custom text instructions to the AI if needed, and let it generate a complete conflict-free schedule for you!
  </Step>
</Steps>
