Festie automatically generates print-ready media for your festival based on the data you enter.

## Downloading Candidate ID Cards (Chest Cards)

ID cards include the candidate's name, chest number, team, your festival's logo, and a unique QR code that links directly to their public profile on the festival website.

1. Navigate to `Subfest > Candidates`.
2. Select the candidates you want to generate cards for (you can select all).
3. Click the **More Options** menu or the download action and select **Download Chest Card**.
4. Choose an available ID card template.
5. Wait for the system to prepare the PDF, then download and print.

*Note: If your festival logo is missing, ensure you have uploaded it in the Festival Settings!*

## Downloading Judgement Sheets & Participant Lists

Before a programme begins, you can print official sheets for your judges and organizers.

1. Navigate to `Subfest > Programmes`.
2. Select a programme and click **Download**.
3. You will see several options:
   - **Participant List:** A list of everyone participating (Group lists are formatted differently than Individual lists).
   - **Judgement Sheet:** A blank scoring sheet pre-filled with candidate chest numbers.
   - **Certificates:** Blank certificates of merit or participation.
   - **Posters:** Promotional posters for the event (e.g., "First Grade", "With Grade", etc.).
4. Select what you need, and the PDF will be generated instantly.
