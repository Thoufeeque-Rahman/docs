---
title: "Judgement sheets and call letters"
description: "Download participant lists, judgement sheets, and call letters."
---

Use programme downloads to prepare printed material for the event.

## Prerequisites

- Programmes and registrations are ready.
- Templates are available for the document type you want to download.

Open `Subfest > Programme`, then select a programme.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open programme downloads">
    Use the download action from the programme page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a template">
    Select participant list, individual judgement sheet, group judgement sheet, or another available template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the file">
    Click download and wait for the generated file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate call letters">
    Use **Generate call letter** when you need call letters for participants.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Add custom templates under `Subfest > Templates` before downloading if the default layout does not match your festival format.
</Tip>
