---
title: "Point adjustments"
description: "Add bonus or penalty points for teams and candidates."
---

Point adjustments let you add or subtract points outside the normal result flow.

## Prerequisites

- Teams and candidates exist.
- You know the reason for the adjustment.

Open `Subfest > Results > Point Adjustment`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the target">
    Select whether the adjustment applies to a team, a candidate, or both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the record">
    Choose the candidate or team. Link a programme if the adjustment belongs to a specific programme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose add or subtract">
    Select whether points should be added or removed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter points and reason">
    Add the point value and a reason for the adjustment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the adjustment">
    Save and verify the points update.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Point adjustments can change rankings. Review toppers after saving an adjustment.
</Warning>
