---
title: "Results Entry"
description: "Enter positions and grades for programmes manually or via judge scores."
---

Festie supports three types of result entry: **Manual Result**, **Manual Score**, and **Online Judgement**. 

## 1. Manual Result (Fastest)
Use this if your judges have already calculated everything on paper, and you just need to enter who came 1st, 2nd, and 3rd.

1. Navigate to `Subfest > Results`.
2. Select a programme and ensure its judgement type is set to **Manual Result**.
3. You will see a list of registered candidates.
4. Simply select the Position (e.g., First) and Grade (e.g., A Grade) for the winning candidates.
5. Click **Save Result**.

## 2. Manual Score (Calculated)
Use this if you want the system to calculate the final positions based on raw scores given by multiple judges.

1. Navigate to `Subfest > Results`.
2. Select a programme and change its judgement type to **Manual Score**.
3. Click **Manage Judges** and add your judges (e.g., Judge A, Judge B, Judge C).
4. Enter the maximum possible score for each judge (e.g., Out of 10 or 20).
5. For each candidate, enter the raw score given by each judge.
6. Click **Save Changes** and then click **Generate Result**.
7. Festie will automatically calculate the totals and assign 1st, 2nd, and 3rd positions!

<Note>
  Saving a result does **not** automatically publish it to the public website. You must use the Result Release feature to make it public.
</Note>
