---
title: "Registrations"
description: "Register individual and group programme participants."
---

Use registrations to connect candidates to programmes.

## Prerequisites

- Candidates and programmes are created.
- Registration is open for team managers, or you are registering as admin.

Open `Subfest > Registrations`.

## Individual registration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a programme">
    Open the programme that needs registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click register">
    Select **Register**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search candidates">
    Search and select the candidate from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the registration">
    Save and confirm the candidate appears in the programme.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Group registration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the group programme">
    Choose the programme and team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select members">
    Select the required number of candidates. Use search to find candidates outside the first visible list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review current members">
    Check the current members list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update members if needed">
    Use add or remove actions to change group members.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Group programmes enforce the minimum and maximum member counts configured on the programme.
</Note>
