---
title: "Sections"
description: "Create classifications such as class, stream, or department and assign candidates."
---

Sections let you group candidates beyond team and category. A madrasah can use sections for classes such as Class 1, Class 2, and Class 3.

## Prerequisites

- Candidates are added or imported.
- You know the classifications you want to track.

Open `Subfest > Candidate > Sections`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a classification">
    Add a classification such as `Class`, `Stream`, or `Department`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add section values">
    Add values such as `Class 1`, `Class 2`, or `Class 3`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign candidates">
    Use **Add candidate** and select candidates for each section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat for other sections">
    Add candidates to each class, stream, or department as needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Sections are useful for filtering reports and understanding participation by class or department.
</Note>
