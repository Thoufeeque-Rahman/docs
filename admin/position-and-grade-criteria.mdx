---
title: "Position and grade criteria"
description: "Configure points for positions and grades."
---

Criteria control how points are calculated for programmes.

## Prerequisites

- You have admin access.
- You know the point system used by your festival.

## Position criteria

Position criteria define points for ranks such as first, second, third, or fourth.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open criteria setup">
    Open the criteria area from the dashboard setup task.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit a criterion">
    Update the name and each rank's points. For example, use 5 points for first, 3 for second, and 1 for third.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add another rank">
    Add fourth place or any other rank if your festival uses it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the default">
    Mark the criterion as default when it should apply to new programmes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Grade criteria

Grade criteria define points and percentage thresholds for grades.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open grade criteria">
    Go to the grade criteria area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set grade points">
    Add the points each grade should award.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set percentage thresholds">
    Enter the percentage required for each grade.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Programmes can enable or disable position and grade scoring in their score settings.
</Note>
