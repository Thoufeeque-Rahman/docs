Billing controls the plan used for the festival or standalone subfest.

## Prerequisites

- You are the owner or primary user.
- You know whether you want to continue from trial to a paid plan.

Open `Festival > Billing` or `Subfest > Billing`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the current plan">
    Check whether you are on trial or a paid plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an upgrade">
    Select the plan that fits your madrasah festival.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply a coupon">
    Enter a coupon code if your group or Festie team shared one with you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    Continue to the payment option and pay using the shown method.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share payment proof if requested">
    If the flow asks for WhatsApp confirmation, share the payment details with the Festie team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Coupon codes can differ for giveaway winners, group offers, and other campaigns.
</Note>
