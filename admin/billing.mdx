When you create a new festival, it starts on the default **Trial Plan**. To unlock full features and increase your limits, you will need to upgrade to a paid subscription.

## Prerequisites

- You are the owner or an admin of the festival.
- You have access to a payment method that supports QR code scanning (UPI, etc.).

## Upgrading Your Plan

To change your plan or purchase an upgrade, navigate to `Festival > Billing` from the sidebar. 

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select an Upgrade">
    On the billing page, click the **Subscribe Now** or **Upgrade** button. You will see three main plans:
    - **Basic Plan:** Ideal for normal, single-event festivals at schools or colleges.
    - **Pro Plan:** Required if you are hosting multiple Sub Fests under one main festival.
    - **Ultra Plan:** For large-scale or multi-level festivals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Billing Address & Phone Number">
    Provide your billing address. You can use the default festival address or check the option to provide a custom billing address. **Ensure you include a valid phone number.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply a Coupon Code">
    If you received a discount coupon code from the Festie team, enter it here and click **Apply** to see your discounted total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the Payment via QR Code">
    Click **Continue**. The page will show that your subscription is "Pending" and display a **Payment QR Code**. 
    Scan this code with your payment app and complete the transaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm via WhatsApp">
    After paying, click the **"I have Paid"** button. This will redirect you to WhatsApp to send a confirmation message to the Festie support team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Approval">
    Our team will verify the payment and approve your pending subscription. Once approved, refresh the page to see your new plan active!
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Limits and Add-ons

Once your plan is active, the Billing page will display your **Usage Limits**, such as the maximum number of candidates and programmes allowed.

If you reach your limit (for example, you have 300 candidates but need to add 100 more):
1. Click on **Add-ons**.
2. Select the amount of additional quota you need (e.g., +100 Candidates).
3. Proceed to checkout and complete the payment just like a regular subscription.

Your usage statistics (how many candidates, users, and programmes you are currently using) are always visible on the Billing page so you can track your limits.
