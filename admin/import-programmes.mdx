---
title: "Import Programmes"
description: "Bulk import your competition list using Excel."
---

If you have a large list of programmes for your festival, you can bulk import them using Festie's Excel import feature, saving you the time of adding them one-by-one.

## Prerequisites

- Categories and Criteria must already be configured.
- Your programme list is ready in a spreadsheet.

Navigate to `Subfest > Programmes` and click the **Import** button.

---

## The Import Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the Sample Excel">
    Download the sample Excel file from the import page. This ensures you have the correct column headers required by Festie.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill the Spreadsheet">
    Add your programme codes, names, types, categories, judgement types, and other settings into the corresponding columns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the File">
    Drag and drop your completed spreadsheet into the upload area. If any columns aren't automatically mapped, you can manually map them using the dropdowns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and Edit">
    The system will display a preview of all the programmes you are about to import. 
    You can click into the fields right on this preview screen to correct any mistakes, adjust missing data, or delete invalid rows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the Import">
    Click **Save**. You will see a success message (e.g., "60 programmes saved") and the new programmes will instantly populate in your list.
  </Step>
</Steps>
