---
title: "Topic registration"
description: "Enable and approve topics for programmes that require titles or subjects."
---

Topic registration is useful when a programme requires a title, topic, or description before the event.

## Prerequisites

- The programme exists.
- Candidate registration exists for that programme.

Open `Subfest > Programme`, then open programme settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable topic registration">
    In registration settings, turn on required topic registration for the programme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open topic registration">
    Set topic registration to open if team managers should submit topics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply settings">
    Preview and apply the settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review submitted topics">
    Go to `Subfest > Registrations > Topic Registration`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or approve topics">
    Add a topic and description, or approve a topic submitted by a team manager.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Admins can manage topics even when topic registration is closed to team managers.
</Note>
