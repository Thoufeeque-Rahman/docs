---
title: "Programmes"
description: "Create individual and group programmes with scoring and registration settings."
---

Programmes are the competitions inside a subfest.

## Prerequisites

- Categories, criteria, teams, and candidates are prepared.
- You know whether each programme is individual or group.

Open `Subfest > Programme`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a programme">
    Click the create programme option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add basic details">
    Enter the programme code, name, type, category, and description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose judgement type">
    Select manual result, manual score, or online judgement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set registration details">
    Choose whether registration opens now or later. Configure substitutions and participation limits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure participation limits">
    For individual programmes, set participants per team. For group programmes, set groups per team and members per group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure score settings">
    Enable or disable position and grade scoring for the programme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the programme">
    Create the programme and confirm it appears in the programme list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Manual result means admins enter ranks. Manual score means admins enter judge scores. Online judgement means judges enter scores from their judge panel.
</Note>
