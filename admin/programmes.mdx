title: "Programmes & Settings"
description: "Create and configure individual or group programmes (competitions) with detailed rules."
---

Programmes represent the actual competitions happening in your festival (e.g., Essay Writing, 100m Dash). Because every competition has unique rules, Festie provides extensive settings to configure them.

## Prerequisites

- Categories and Teams are set up.
- You know the specific rules for the competition (limits, scoring, team participation).

Navigate to `Subfest > Programmes` to view, add, or import your programmes.

---

## Adding a Programme Manually

Click the **Add Programme** button. You will go through several configuration tabs:

### 1. Basic Details
- **Programme Code:** A unique identifier for the programme. For example, a Junior Song might be `JN-01` or `F01`.
- **Programme Name:** The official name of the item.
- **Programme Type:** Choose **Individual** (candidates participate alone) or **Group** (candidates participate as a team of 3, 4, etc.).
- **Judgement Type:** How will the results be recorded?
  - *Manual Result:* Admins directly input who came 1st, 2nd, 3rd.
  - *Manual Score:* Admins input the raw scores given by the judges on paper, and the system calculates the result.
  - *Online Judgement:* Judges use a separate online portal to enter scores directly on their phones.
- **Category:** The category this programme belongs to.
- **Collections & Skills:** Group the programme into Collections (like "Stage" or "Off-stage") and tag it with Skills (like "Language" or "Athletics") for deep analytics later.
- **Concept Note:** A brief explanation of what the programme is about.

### 2. Registration Settings
- **Open Registration:** Choose if registration is open immediately.
- **Topic Registration:** Enable this if candidates must register a specific topic (e.g., Speech Topic) prior to the event.
- **Substitution Rules:** Define if team managers are allowed to swap candidates later due to medical or other reasons.

### 3. Participant Settings (Curbs & Limits)
- **Min/Max Limits:** Set the minimum and maximum number of candidates/groups a team can field for this programme. For no limit, leave them as `0`.
- **Team Eligibility:** By default, "All Teams can participate" is checked. If this is a restricted event, uncheck it and specify which teams are allowed.
- **Category Eligibility:** By default, only candidates from the selected Category can participate. For "General" events where anyone can join, uncheck this box and specify the allowed categories.

### 4. Score Settings
- Enable or disable **Position Criteria** and **Grade Criteria** based on whether this event awards them.
- **Point Percentages:** You can control how many points are actually awarded. If a 1st place gives 10 points, but you want the candidate to receive 0 points (perhaps it's a fun event), you can set the *Candidate Point Percentage* to `0%`. You can adjust this for both Candidate and Team percentages independently.

### 5. Call Letter Settings
- **Generation Mode:** Choose whether the system should generate call letters using Alphabets (A, B, C) or Numbers (1, 2, 3).
- **Custom Order:** For certain sports or track events, you may need teams to perform in a specific order (e.g., Alpha Team first, Blue Team second). You can manually set the team appearance order here.

Click **Save** once you've configured the settings. The programme will appear in your list.
