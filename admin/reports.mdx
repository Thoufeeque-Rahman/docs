description: "Use reports to review participation, results, curbs, toppers, and exports."
---

Reports help you audit the festival during and after the event.

## Prerequisites

- Relevant setup and event data is entered.
- Templates exist if you plan to download formatted documents.

Open `Subfest > Reports`.

Use reports for:

- Curbs and participation limits.
- Programme results.
- Candidate points.
- Toppers.
- Programme registrations.
- Programme participation.
- Topic registrations.
- Substitutions.
- Full programme and candidate reports.
- Exports.

<Tip>
  Reports become more useful after candidates, programmes, registrations, schedules, and results are entered.
</Tip>
