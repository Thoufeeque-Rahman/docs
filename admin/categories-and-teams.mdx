---
title: "Categories and teams"
description: "Set participant categories and team details before adding candidates."
---

Categories group candidates by level. Teams group candidates for registrations, scoring, and reports.

## Prerequisites

- You have admin access to the subfest.
- You know the categories and teams used in your festival.

## Categories

Open the category setup area from the dashboard task or the relevant settings area.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review default categories">
    Check the categories created with the madrasah setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rename a category">
    Edit any default category, such as renaming `Sub Junior` to `Bidaya`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add missing categories">
    Add each extra category your festival needs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Teams

Open the team setup area from the dashboard task or `Subfest > Candidate > Teams`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review existing teams">
    Check the default teams such as Yellow or Green.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit team details">
    Update the team name, color, logo, and related details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create new teams">
    Add all teams before candidate registration starts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Team colors and logos help admins and team managers identify registrations and results faster.
</Note>
