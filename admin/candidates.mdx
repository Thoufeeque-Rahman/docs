---
title: "Candidates"
description: "Add candidates one by one and assign their team and category."
---

Candidates are the students or participants who compete in programmes.

## Prerequisites

- Categories and teams are configured.
- Chest number rules are ready.

Open `Subfest > Candidate`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a candidate">
    Click the create candidate option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter identity details">
    Add the chest number if manual, then enter the candidate name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload an image">
    Add the candidate image if available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose team and category">
    Select the candidate's team and category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the candidate">
    Submit the form and verify the candidate appears in the candidate list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Candidate profile pages show details, programmes, results, point adjustments, gallery items, and related activity.
</Note>
