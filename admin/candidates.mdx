---
title: "Candidate Entry"
description: "Add candidates (participants) to your festival manually."
---

Candidates are the students or participants who will be competing in the programmes. There are two ways to add candidates in Festie: you can add them one-by-one, or you can bulk import them using an Excel file.

## Prerequisites

- Categories and Teams are properly configured.
- Chest number rules have been decided (whether manual or auto-generated).

---

## Adding Candidates Manually

To add a candidate one-by-one, go to `Subfest > Candidates` and click the **Add Candidate** button.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Chest Number">
    Enter the candidate's Chest Number. If you have "Chest Number Auto Generation" enabled in the settings, this field will be filled automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Basic Details">
    Add the required and optional identity details:
    - **Admission / Register Number:** Institutional ID of the student.
    - **Name:** The full name of the candidate.
    - **Date of Birth & Gender:** Optional demographics.
    - **Email & Contact:** For communication purposes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Candidate Avatar">
    Upload a photo or avatar of the candidate from your device. Once uploaded, the avatar will be visible on their profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Team and Category">
    Assign the candidate to their respective **Team** (e.g., Red Team) and **Category** (e.g., Junior).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Academic Details">
    Provide their Class and Stream (if applicable). Note that you can configure and change class/stream options in the settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The candidate will now successfully appear in your Candidates list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Once candidates are added, their profile pages will display their specific programmes, results, point adjustments, and gallery items.
</Note>
