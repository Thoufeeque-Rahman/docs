After creating your festival, you will be taken to the Dashboard. This page serves as your central command center, guiding you through the setup process and giving you an overview of your festival's progress.

## Global Navigation

At the top of the interface and in the sidebar, you have several global tools available:
- **Search:** Quickly search for any feature, page, or setting in the app.
- **Refer & Earn:** Share Festie with other institutions or schools and earn rewards.
- **AI Assistant:** An integrated AI option where you can ask any questions regarding Festie and get immediate help.
- **Install App:** You have the option to install Festie as a Desktop or Mobile app (PWA) directly from your browser.
- **Documentation Videos:** Embedded tutorial videos appear at the top to guide you through each feature.

## Sidebar Menu Overview

The left sidebar gives you access to the main sections of your festival:
- **Festival Page:** Where you handle data entry, result entry, and overall management for the specific festival.
- **Users:** Manage who has access to your festival. You can add owners, share ownership, assign roles, and manage permissions.
- **Website:** Customize the design and page visibility of your public-facing festival website.
- **Billing:** View your current subscription plan and explore upgrade options (like upgrading from Basic to Pro).
- **Settings:** Configure overall festival settings, update its name and profile, and enable or disable specific features.
- **Notifications:** Check system alerts, referral updates, and any direct notifications.

## Account & Preferences

At the very bottom of the sidebar, clicking on your **User Icon** opens your account management menu:
- **Switch/Create Festivals:** Easily jump between different festivals or create a new one.
- **Manage Account:** Update your personal profile and change your password.
- **Preferences (Theme & Accessibility):**
  - Toggle between **Dark Mode** and **Light Mode**.
  - Adjust the **Font Size** for better readability.
  - Change the overall accent **Color** (e.g., Blue, Red) across the entire application.
- **Account History:** View payment history and login activities.

## Dashboard Setup Checklist

The Dashboard itself acts as a checklist, showing you exactly what to configure next. Here is the recommended order for setting up your festival:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Categories & Teams">
    Add and update the Categories (e.g., Sub Junior, Senior) and manage the Teams (e.g., Red House, Blue House) that will participate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Position & Grade Criteria">
    Define how points are awarded for different positions (1st, 2nd, 3rd) and grades (A, B, C).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chest Number Rules">
    Set up the logic for automatically generating and assigning chest numbers to candidates as they are added.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Candidates & Programmes">
    Enter the participant data (Candidates) and configure the events/items (Programmes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Curbs (Limits)">
    Add any rules or limits, such as restricting a single candidate to participating in a maximum of 5 programmes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Always return to the dashboard after completing a section. It will automatically update to show your progress and guide you to the next setup task.
</Tip>
