---
title: "Result release"
description: "Publish or unpublish groups of programme results."
---

Create a release when you want to publish one or more programme results.

## Prerequisites

- Results are entered and reviewed.
- You are ready for the selected results to appear publicly.

Open `Subfest > Results > Release`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a release">
    Choose the option to publish results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose publish mode">
    Select publish or unpublish mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the release">
    Use a clear name such as `Morning release`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select programmes">
    Select the programmes to include. Watch the side panel update team points and toppers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the release">
    Confirm the release when the programme list and point changes are correct.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Publishing makes selected results available on the website and affects visible points and toppers.
</Warning>
