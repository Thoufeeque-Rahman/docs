---
title: "Result Release"
description: "Publish grouped results to your public website."
---

Once results are entered, they remain private. The **Result Release** feature allows you to bundle several results together and publish them to the public website at once.

## Prerequisites

- Results must be entered and saved for the programmes you wish to publish.

Navigate to `Subfest > Results > Release`.

---

## Publishing Results

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start Publishing">
    Click **Start Publishing Results** and choose whether you want to Publish or Unpublish results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the Release">
    Give your release a descriptive name (e.g., `Morning Session Results` or `Release 1`). 
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Programmes">
    The system will show you a list of all programmes that have results ready but are not yet published. Select the ones you want to include in this release.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview Live Changes">
    As you select programmes, look at the sidebar! Festie will dynamically show you how these specific results will affect the overall Team Points and who will become the new Candidate Toppers. 
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft or Publish">
    - Click **Save Draft** if you want to prepare the release but publish it later.
    - Click **Publish Result** to immediately push it live to the website.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Unpublishing Results
If a mistake was made, you can start a new Release, select "Unpublish", and choose the incorrectly published programme to instantly remove its results from the public website.
