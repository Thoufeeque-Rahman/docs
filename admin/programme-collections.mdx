---
title: "Programme collections"
description: "Group programmes into collections such as Stage and Non-stage."
---

Collections let you group programmes for limits, filtering, and reporting.

## Prerequisites

- Programmes are created or imported.
- You know the collections used by your festival.

Open `Subfest > Programme > Collections`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a collection">
    Add a collection such as `Stage` or `Non-stage`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add programmes">
    Select programmes and add them to the collection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the collection">
    Confirm the correct programmes are included.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Create collections before curbs if your limits apply only to a type of programme, such as stage programmes.
</Tip>
