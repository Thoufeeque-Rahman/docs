description: "Manage who has access to your festival, create custom roles, and assign team managers."
---

You can delegate festival management by adding other users and assigning them specific roles, like Team Managers, Judges, or Volunteers. 

## Prerequisites

- The user you want to invite must have already created a Festie account using their email address.
- If you are adding a Team Manager, your Teams must already be created.

Navigate to `Festival > Users` (or from the dashboard sidebar). You will see three main sections: **Festival Owners**, **Roles**, and **Users**.

---

## 1. Festival Owners
If you created the festival, you are the primary owner. If you want to give another person full administrative ownership:
1. Click **Add Owner**.
2. Enter their registered Festie email address.
3. Once added, they will appear as a "Shared Owner" and have the exact same full access as you do.

---

## 2. Managing Roles & Custom Roles
Festie comes with several default roles: *Admin*, *Team Manager*, *Judge*, *Media*, and *Volunteer*. 

If you need a specific type of access, you can create a **Custom Role**.
For example, if you want a volunteer whose ONLY job is to manage Candidate data:
1. Go to the **Roles** tab and click **Create Role**.
2. Name it (e.g., `Candidate Volunteer`).
3. Under permissions, grant *Read* and *Write* access strictly to the `Candidates` module, and leave everything else blank.
4. Save the role. Users assigned this role will not be able to see or edit anything other than Candidates.

---

## 3. Adding Users & Team Managers
To invite people to help run the festival, go to the **Users** tab (you can also access this via `Settings > Users`).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add User">
    Click **Add User** and enter their registered email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Role">
    Choose the role they should have (e.g., *Team Manager*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Team (For Team Managers)">
    If you selected *Team Manager*, a new option will appear asking which team they manage. Select their specific team (e.g., *Green Team*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant Access">
    Click **Add Access**. The user will now have permission.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The Team Manager Dashboard Experience

When a user assigned as a **Team Manager** logs into Festie, they will see a completely different, restricted dashboard compared to an Admin.
- They will only see options for Candidate Registration, Topic Registration, Substitutions, and Appeals.
- They will **only** see the data and candidates belonging to their specific assigned team (e.g., Green Team). They cannot view or manage candidates from other teams.
