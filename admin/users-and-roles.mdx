---
title: "Users and roles"
description: "Add team managers and assign them to teams."
---

Use users and roles to let team managers register their own participants.

## Prerequisites

- The user has already created a Festie account.
- Teams are configured.

Open `Subfest > Users`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask the user to create an account">
    Tell the team manager to sign in or create an account using the email you will add.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the user">
    Click **Add user** and enter the email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the role">
    Choose the team manager role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the team">
    Select the team this manager should control.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant access">
    Save the user access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Team managers can view their team details, register candidates, view results, and manage the areas allowed for their role.
</Note>
