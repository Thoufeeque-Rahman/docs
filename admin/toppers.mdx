Toppers show leaders based on published or total result data.

## Topper placeholders

A topper placeholder is a saved leaderboard block in a subfest. It defines what
kind of topper list Festie should prepare or show, such as top candidates, top
teams, or top sections.

The placeholder is the configuration. Ranking entries are the actual rows inside
that placeholder, such as first place, second place, points, programme count,
candidate, team, section, or category details.

Use topper placeholders when you want the same leaderboard to be regenerated,
reviewed, filtered, or connected to public website sections without rebuilding
the leaderboard setup each time.

## Ranking entries

Ranking entries are generated or maintained under a topper placeholder. They
represent the current standings for that placeholder. For example, a Top
Candidates placeholder can contain candidate rows with rank, points, programme
count, and category context.

If a topper placeholder has no ranking entries yet, it means the leaderboard
block exists but Festie has no rows to show for it. This can happen before
results are entered, before results are published, or before toppers are
regenerated.

## Prerequisites

- Results are entered.
- Result releases are published if you want public topper standings.

Open `Subfest > Results > Toppers`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review published toppers">
    Check toppers based on published results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use total toppers when needed">
    Switch to total toppers to see standings that include entered results before publishing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter and search">
    Use rank, limit, and search options to narrow the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Regenerate if requested">
    If Festie shows that regeneration is needed, click regenerate to update topper data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Published toppers and total toppers may differ until all results are released.
</Note>
